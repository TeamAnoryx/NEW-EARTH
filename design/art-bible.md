# NEW EARTH — Art Bible

Reference images are supplied by the project owner (station exterior, bunk-room breach, corridor outbreak, three character sheets).

## Exterior
Large habitat ring seen from a high three-quarter angle over a cloud-swirled Earth. A central deck crosses the ring with a tall spire and a red lab module (the breach). Warm window bands, blinking nav lights and orbiting craft.

## Interiors
- **Crew quarters:** pale grey panelled walls with bolts, hazard stripes and screens. Cool ceiling light. The glass door is the focal point: blood, cracks and pounding hands.
- **Corridor:** dark industrial hull, red emergency light, haze and sparks, blood on the floor and walls, red-lit lab windows.
- **Storage C-7:** dim and amber, with racks, crates, EVA lockers and a workbench. A sickly green spill comes through the jammed door.

## Characters
Stylised 3D, with silhouettes and colour blocks read from the reference sheets (see `game-brief.md`). The infected wear grey, torn clothing and blood-stained lab coats. Their eyes are milky white (they are blind).

## Post-processing
ACES tone mapping, restrained bloom on emissives only, mild chromatic aberration, vignette and film grain. Red danger pulse under threat.

## Known gap vs. references
Everything is procedural (primitives and canvas textures), so it runs offline with no asset files. It matches the references' layout, palette and silhouettes, not their photoreal detail. Reaching that level needs rigged, textured GLB character models and mocap animation clips, swapped in for the procedural rig.
