# NEW EARTH — Game Brief (Prologue: Outbreak)

**Genre:** single-player third-person survival / stealth · **Platform:** browser (offline single-file build; online later)

## Premise
HELIOS-7, a ring research station orbiting Earth. A pathogen escapes Lab Deck B. The infected are **blind** and hunt by **sound**; they spread by bite/contact. The outbreak started in the lab and is spreading through the station.

## Prologue beats (built)
1. **Wake** — night in Crew Quarters 3-C; alarm; Max wakes, wakes Sean and Anna.
2. **The door** — through the glass, the infected feeding; one hears them and screams; QTE to seal the door.
3. **Search** — glass is cracking on a timer; search the room; find the **nav-pad** (station map) under the corner bench.
4. **Vent** — the map shows Storage C-7 directly below; pry the floor grate; crawl the duct.
5. **Storage** — door ajar, infected standing silent outside; sneak (crouch), hold E to slide it shut, engage the lock.
6. **Regroup** — the three sit and plan to gather equipment. End of prologue.

## Core mechanic
Noise. Sprint = loud, walk = medium, crouch = near-silent. Perceived noise falls off with distance and the open door gap. Over the threshold, the infected rush.
